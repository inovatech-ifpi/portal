**INOVATECH** is an intensive public-sector software engineering residency led by the **Federal Institute of Piauí (IFPI Campus Teresina Central)** in strategic partnership with **Piauí Gov Tech / ETIPI** (State of Piauí Digital Transformation Agency).

Fifteen selected engineering fellows, funded with 20-hour weekly stipends, work in multidisciplinary squads to solve real, mission-critical bottlenecks for public institutions and state government agencies.

## What Sets the Model Apart

Fellows do not build toy prototypes or academic sandboxes: they ship production software. Every track addresses an authenticated public bottleneck, operates under real regulatory constraints, and concludes with a formal **7-Gate Institutional Handover** (named civil service maintainer, operational runbooks delivered, audited rollback plans, and signed transfer agreements).

## Web Summit Engagement & Partnerships

At Web Summit, we invite global and institutional partners to collaborate across three engagement models:

1. **Institutional Challenge Sponsor (Bring a Problem):** A public agency presents a verified operational bottleneck with dedicated domain owners. INOVATECH mobilizes an elite engineering squad guided by senior academic instructors through formal handover.
2. **Assisted Operations & Network Scaling Partner:** Provide infrastructure, cloud credits, or operational support to scale SGAE, Flix, or Conecta across federal networks and state agencies.
3. **Sponsor an International Fellowship Cohort:** Fund stipends to expand our proven GovTech talent accelerator from 15 to 50+ young software fellows.
